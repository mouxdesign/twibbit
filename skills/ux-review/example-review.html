Part 1: Framework review

The Twibbit 10 principles. Principles that don't apply are marked.

1. Mistakes are permanentPrevent errors before they happen. Doesn't apply here. No payment is sent.
2. Trust is everythingEvery screen answers: is my money safe and is everything okay?
3. Onboarding is a crisis pointThe first run is when people make serious mistakes. See change 3.
4. Jargon kills adoptionEvery jargon word is a person who gives up. See changes 1, 4 and 5.
5. Progressive disclosureShow the simple version first. Doesn't apply here.
6. Extreme user rangeWork for beginners and experts. See change 4.
7. Invisible tech, visible stateAlways show what is happening now. See changes 2 and 3.
8. Security vs usabilityConfirmations should earn their place. Doesn't apply here.
9. Education is part of the UXThe app has to teach as people go. See change 1.
10. Password protectionNobody can reset a lost seed phrase. Doesn't apply here.

First impressions

The words are short and direct. That's good. Three of them stop a newcomer: Lightning channel, sat and Payer. The tone isn't consistent. The notice is a full sentence. The labels are single words.

Two things I can't check from a screenshot. The orange labels and grey hints on the near black background may be hard to read. Check the contrast. I also can't tell what the spinner is waiting for.

Privacy: “Payer” and “Message” suggest the other person may see what users type. Confirm what is shared. Then say it on the screen.

Part 2: Bitcoin Design Guide

Receiving

To receive over Lightning, users need a channel with enough room. The Guide says any fee for opening one should be shown when the request is created, before it is shared. Your screen mentions the channel. It doesn't mention the fee. See change 1. Receiving ↗

Explaining channels

The Blixt case study says newcomers are missing the “why”. Saying how long opening a channel takes sets a clear expectation. Keep the text short. Many users aren't native English speakers. See change 1. Blixt case study ↗

Units

For Lightning wallets the Guide suggests satoshi as the default unit. Users should be able to pick their own unit and see local currency easily. Your screen already shows sats and dollars. See change 4. Units and symbols ↗
