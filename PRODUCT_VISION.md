# Twibbit: Product Vision

AI agents build bitcoin wallets people don't trust. Twibbit gives them the UX know-how to build apps people love to use.

In interviews, 7 of 10 bitcoin builders saw their AI get it wrong.

## Two years from now….

Every bitcoin wallet builder using AI has a product experience sidekick. That's Twibbit. Twibbit becomes such a valuable organic part of their builder process that it just gets used by default.

Twibbit is now being recommended to their peers just because they see so much value in it. Bitcoin builders are able to build faster with better UX design. They're able to iterate quickly, build faster, and build more efficient, more beautiful user experiences. They get their product to market faster and build a loyal user and fan base. Support questions drop because the app is intuitive. They stand out in a crowded market with many well-financed competitors, and they achieve their vision without feeling held back.

This rolls over into the user experience. Users are landing on their apps. They feel a complete difference when using them because the experience just feels so much easier to use and more importantly they feel safer and trust is increased in the bitcoin brand itself.

In the end, builders end up with a more efficient workflow and they also end up getting more users and more user retention.

## The problems

When an agent builds an app it does not have the innate knowledge that a UX designer has to design a smooth, empowering user experience. What's more, it does not understand the nuances that are inherent in bitcoin that we have to pay attention to. Trust being the biggest thing. In the interviews, 7 of 10 bitcoin builders saw their AI get it wrong. In their own words:

> "Sometimes it just made up, hallucinated and I had to give it the documentation to see how it was supposed to build it." (Builder 4)

> "We added in a button and we forgot to add a dark mode variant to the button. So when the user goes to dark mode, the button isn't going to be readable." (Builder 1)

> "And for UI, it's basically useless." (Builder 9)

A person is not going to place their life savings or fiat money (daily spending) into something which they don't inherently trust.

In bitcoin trust matters much more because we are dealing with high stakes. Right now people are mostly holding their bitcoin and using it as a savings mechanism.

AI is the UX designer right now and the design aesthetic is developing a distinct AI design feel which can be seen across many apps and websites.

## Who it's for

Twibbit is for bitcoin builders who are primarily building lightning or bitcoin wallets.

These could be:

- Solo builders
- Small teams

Who are primarily using an AI-powered workflow.

## What it is

Twibbit is an open-source UX guide you give to the AI agent you already use. It gives your agent a bitcoin UX designer's knowledge, so it can support you in building an app people love using.

It is a set of skill files. Some help builders to build a brand direction and Jobs to be done from scratch by interviewing them, so that they can have a deeper understanding of who their target audience is. That understanding then shapes the direction of the user experience, which flows out into UI design and copy.

Twibbit is a product and I can help early projects to adopt it as part of the process to learn from them and how they use it.

## What makes it different

Twibbit has inherent user experience knowledge that is very specific to the bitcoin ecosystem. It will use the [Bitcoin Design Guide](https://bitcoin.design/) as a reference point.

It is written by a UX designer who has worked in the bitcoin ecosystem, and it is kept current as the ecosystem changes. Designing for bitcoin is very different to designing for regular user experiences. Trust is the highest aspect that we have to assess and show during the user journey.

An agent can't just point at the Bitcoin Design Guide because pointing their agent at the Bitcoin Design Guide will be too generic based off of the specifications of their project. They would spend a lot of time searching. This is just going to be very specific.

## MVP

The MVP tests one question: does the agent produce better wallet UX with Twibbit than without it?

Step 1, Interview and direction: It's going to start by interviewing them first. The interview is basically a skill file which asks them a series of questions and then decides whether to reroute them in a specific direction. The builder's own AI agent will run it using Twibbit. Twibbit listens for what the project is, who the audience is, and what already exists, and then sends you to one or two skill files that matter most. We want to give them the feeling that they're really being streamlined in a specific direction.

The skill files on day one:

- Jobs to be done: interviews you to understand your target market, then creates a hypothesis of that market's jobs to be done.
- Branding: helps you think about your branding and explore different design directions.
- Copy: analyzes only your copy.

Builders can also choose a skill file first, or go straight to UX review.

Step 2, UX review: builders will also have the option to input a screen and request a UX review. Twibbit will then provide recommendations to them based on the best-in-class UX practices. The MVP will reference where the UX recommendations came from.

## Future roadmap

Step 3: Full product flow analysis. Often a design decision may impact other design decisions made on other screens. One could upload all screens and have Twibbit make an intelligent inference as to which other screens could be affected by a single design decision.

Step 4: Option to input the front-end code of that current screen. Twibbit will then suggest a UX change at the design + code level so they can ship faster.

## How we'll know it's working

We'll know Twibbit is working if 5 projects are using it.

We'll know it's successful by the feedback that builders provide. We want to collect testimonials from builders who have used it to show real use.

Before/after: When they produce actual designs, copy, branding using Twibbit we will showcase before and after screenshots showing the impact. Examples are anonymized so no project is singled out.
